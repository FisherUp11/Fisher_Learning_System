Composition optimized for a top-down 2D game overlay: most detailed narrative scenery along top 25 percent and outside edges, large central and lower 65 percent calm low-detail mid-value blue-green lake with extremely subtle ripple texture, no high-contrast central ornament, enough empty space for cream poetry cards and cyan-white/coral-red toy tanks. DO NOT render any tanks, characters, soldiers, text, letters, Chinese calligraphy, UI, game HUD, frame, watermark, or logo. Full-bleed landscape artwork, not a screenshot or mockup.
